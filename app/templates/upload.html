What is a TLE File?

A Two-Line Element set (TLE) is a standardized format used by NASA, NORAD, and space agencies worldwide to describe the orbit of an Earth-orbiting object. Each record consists of a name line followed by two lines of orbital parameters.

Example TLE — International Space Station (ISS)
ISS (ZARYA)
1 25544U 98067A   24001.50000000  .00002182  00000-0  47972-4 0  9999
2 25544  51.6416 247.4627 0006703 130.5360 325.0288 15.50377579430900
